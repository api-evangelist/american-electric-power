AEP Texas, a subsidiary of American Electric Power, has entered into an agreement for a loan of up to $3.26 billion from the U.S. Department of Energy’s Office of Energy Dominance Financing to help fund infrastructure investments to improve resiliency and deliver an estimated $685 million in total savings to customers over 30 years.
